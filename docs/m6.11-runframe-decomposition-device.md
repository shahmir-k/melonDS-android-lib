# M6.11 step 2 — RunFrame decomposition (on-device, Anbernic RG DS / Cortex-A55)

**Goal:** pin down the *absolute* A55 ms/frame of each `NDS::RunFrame()` bucket
in-race, so the GPU3D geometry engine can be sized before the NEON geometry work
(M6.11). Step 1 (`m6.11-runframe-decomposition-host.md`) established the *ratios*
on an Apple M3; this doc measures the *absolute times* on the target silicon.

## Device / method

- **Device:** Anbernic RG DS, RK3568, 4× Cortex-A55 @ 1.992 GHz, Android 14 (API
  34), rooted (`adb shell su 0`). Serial `e0ca3841840f2ab9`.
- **Binary:** `liteDS-headless`, cross-built for `arm64-v8a` with
  **`LITEV_PROFILE=ON`** in two configs (build via
  `LITEV_PROFILE=ON BUILD_TAG=-prof tools/android-bench/build.sh baseline full`,
  an additive `LITEV_PROFILE`/`BUILD_TAG` env override, both defaulting to the
  pre-existing behaviour — kept as a **local, uncommitted** working change per
  the measurement-only discipline; the two-line `build.sh` delta is reproducible
  from the cmake config matrix already in that script):
  - **baseline** — no LITEV perf flags: the **exact-timing** config that ties
    back to the goldens (`shrek-600.trace`, `shrek-race-3400.trace`).
  - **full** — `JIT_DISPATCH + LINK_* + EVENT_SLICES + MEM_DTCM_BLOCK +
    MEM_MAINRAM_LOAD` all ON: the **app-matching** config (mirrors run-race.sh's
    `full` cell and the shipped app's flag set, Appendix D.3).
- **Workload:** in-race Shrek Quick Race savestate (`shrek-race.mln`, regenerated
  on host from the committed script, SHA-256 `06ee0c39…`, pushed to
  `/data/local/tmp/liteds/`), hold-A input, `--frames 961 --bench-window 60:960
  --frameskip 0 --mode jit --fastmem off`. Window = 901 frames (the
  active-driving portion). Contrast: 600f direct boot, window 60:599.
- **Quiet-device discipline:** `am force-stop me.magnum.melonds.dev` before
  measuring (app was already not running; nothing restored — user can relaunch).
  A55 clock and thermal_zone0 sampled before/after every run.
- **fastmem:** `off`. Fastmem's `ftruncate` mmap fails in the `adb shell`
  domain (`Failed to allocate memory using ftruncate` — a non-fatal notice), so
  `fastmem_active=no` regardless; runs are fm-off and labelled as such.

## Gates (both PASS on-device)

1. **Golden trace, bit-exact on device.** `liteDS-baseline-prof --frames 600
   --verify-trace shrek-600.trace` → **`OK, 600/600 frames identical`** on the
   device. The `LITEV_PROFILE` instrumentation is observational — it does not
   perturb emulation on A55 any more than on host.
2. **Savestate portability + determinism.** The host-regenerated savestate,
   loaded on-device, reproduces the host reference in-race frame hash **exactly**
   (frame 960: top=`5d5920a469598405` bot=`a432f5f7e3b7bfa6`) on every one of the
   6 in-race runs across both configs. Host↔A55 savestate transfer is
   byte-faithful to the frame-hash.

### The RunSystem caveat carries from host to the headless device binary

The headless binary uses the **software renderer** (`ENABLE_OGLRENDERER=OFF`), so
the 2D+3D rasterizer runs on the CPU inside `RunSystem` (`Event_LCD →
DrawScanline`). That is the ~24 ms / 50–60% `RunSystem` bucket. **The shipped app
uses the GL renderer**, so that rasterization runs on the RK3568 GPU, *not* in
`NDS::RunFrame()` on the CPU. Therefore:

- The headless **RunFrame total (40–48 ms) and RunSystem are NOT the app's frame
  time** — they are inflated by the CPU software rasterizer.
- The **emu-compute buckets (ARM9 / GPU3D / ARM7 / DMA)** and the residual *are*
  real A55 absolute times that transfer directly to the app. This is exactly the
  device-relevant picture step 1 said to read.

## Reading

### 1. Absolute geometry size on the A55: ~2.3 ms in-race (app-matching config)

GPU3D geometry drains **8,451 GXFIFO commands/frame** at **271 ns/command** on
the A55 (`full`), totalling **~2.29 ms/frame**. On exact-timing baseline it is
**~2.77 ms** at 328 ns/command. The A55 spends **~8.5–10×** longer per GX command
than the M3 (271–328 ns vs 23–32 ns) — the expected in-order-A55-vs-wide-OoO-M3
gap. The GX command *volume* (8,451/frame) is silicon-independent and matches the
host exactly, confirming the same geometry workload is being measured.

### 2. Where geometry sits in the app's real frame

The app's own profiler reports an in-race **`runFrame` ≈ 18.9 ms** (≈33–35 fps;
`cpu_loop=28.25 ms = runFrame 18.89 + blit 8.24 + other 1.12`, GL renderer so no
software rasterizer inside RunFrame). Our headless **emu-compute + residual**
under the app-matching `full` config is **13.56 + 2.53 = 16.1 ms** — landing just
under the app's 18.9 ms, the remainder being GL-renderer CPU-side setup (vertex
upload / 2D compositor) that the headless SW path books differently, plus the
absence of profiler overhead in the app. The two independent measurements agree
to ~15%, which validates the decomposition against the app.

So **geometry (~2.3 ms) is roughly 12% of the app's ~18.9 ms RunFrame, and ~17%
of pure ARM emulation compute.** It is the **second-largest emu-compute bucket
after ARM9** and — crucially — **ARM9 is already the optimized one** (`full` cut
ARM9 from 11.55→7.55 ms, −35%, via JIT dispatch/linking/event-slices/mem-tier),
which is precisely *why* geometry's *share* of emu-compute rises from 15.0%
(baseline) to 16.9% (full): the denominator shrank around the untouched geometry
slice. **Geometry is the largest untouched in-race emulation slice — the M6.11
premise holds on-device.**

### 3. Projected NEON-geometry payoff ceiling

Geometry math (4×4 matrix × vec4 transforms, lighting dot-products, vertex
interpolation) vectorizes well on NEON; clipping is branchy and vectorizes
poorly. DraStic — the reference for a full-speed DS emulator on weak ARM — gets
its geometry engine to run in hand-written NEON and reports roughly **~2–4×**
over scalar ARM on the transform/lighting inner loops. Taking a blended **~3×**:

- **Amdahl ceiling on the whole bucket:** 2.29 ms × (1 − 1/3) = **~1.5 ms saved**
  (2× → 1.15 ms; 4× → 1.72 ms). This is the optimistic bound that assumes the
  entire GPU3D bucket is the vectorizable math.
- **Realistic:** ~65–75% of the bucket is the vectorizable vertex/matrix/light
  math; the rest is GXFIFO command decode/dispatch that NEON does not touch. At
  3× on that fraction: **~1.0–1.15 ms saved** per frame.

**What that means for the 16.6 ms (60 fps) budget:** perfect geometry NEON pulls
the app's RunFrame from ~18.9 ms to roughly **~17.4–17.9 ms** — a real dent, but
it does **not** by itself reach the 16.6 ms budget (RunFrame is ~2.3 ms over, and
the full `cpu_loop` is ~28 ms once the 8.2 ms blit is counted). The honest
sizing: **geometry NEON is the highest-leverage *untouched* emulation-compute
target (biggest slice nothing has optimized), but its absolute ceiling is
~1–1.7 ms/frame — a meaningful contribution that must be combined with blit
reduction (~8 ms) and further ARM9/renderer work to close the gap to 60 fps.** It
is worth doing on leverage-per-unopted-slice grounds, not because it alone hits
the budget.

---

## Bottom line for M6.11

On the Cortex-A55 in-race, GPU3D geometry is **~2.3 ms/frame** (app-matching
config), driven by **8,451 GXFIFO commands/frame at 271 ns/command**, is the
**#2 emulation-compute consumer behind the already-optimized ARM9**, and is
completely unoptimized. A DraStic-class ~3× NEON pass on the vertex/matrix/clip
math has a **~1.5 ms ceiling / ~1.0–1.2 ms realistic** per-frame payoff — the
largest single win available from untouched emulation code, though not on its own
enough to reach the 16.6 ms 60-fps budget.
